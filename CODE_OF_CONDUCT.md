# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a good place to work:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, review comments — and when an individual is representing the project in
public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/DipeshPansuriya/MLX_Model_App/security/advisories/new)
or by direct message. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.

Possible responses, scaled to severity:

| Severity | Response |
|---|---|
| Minor, first occurrence | Private warning with a request to stop |
| Repeated or moderate | Temporary removal from interaction |
| Severe or sustained | Permanent ban from project spaces |
